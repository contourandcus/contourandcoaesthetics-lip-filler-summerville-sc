About the treatment

What Lip Filler Actually Does

Lip filler is a hyaluronic acid gel placed carefully into the lips. Hyaluronic acid is a substance your body already makes, and in the lips it draws in water, which is why well-placed filler can improve hydration and softness at the same time as it adds shape.

Good lip enhancement is usually about small, deliberate changes rather than obvious volume. Depending on your goals, treatment can focus on defining the lip border, balancing the top and bottom lip, supporting the corners of the mouth, or restoring fullness that has faded with age.

Because the product range for lips includes softer and more structured options, including products made specifically for lips such as Revanesse Lips+, your plan is chosen for your anatomy and the finish you want, not picked from a standard menu.

What Lip Enhancement Can Help With

Most clients in Summerville come in with one or two specific concerns rather than a request for bigger lips. These are the most common ones.

Definition and Shape

A softer lip border can make lipstick bleed and lips look less defined. Filler can sharpen the outline gently, including the cupid's bow, so the shape reads clearly again without looking drawn on.

Balance and Symmetry

Almost everyone has one side slightly fuller than the other. Careful, uneven placement, on purpose, can even out the top and bottom lip and side to side differences for a more balanced result.

Hydration and Fine Lines

Dry, lined lips are often a volume and hydration issue, not just a lip balm issue. Hyaluronic acid filler can smooth vertical lip lines and help lips hold moisture, so they look healthier as well as fuller.

Is it right for you

Who This Treatment Suits Best

You may be a good candidate if any of the following sound familiar:

  • Your lips have thinned over time and lost their shape
  • One lip, or one side, is noticeably smaller than the other
  • Your lip border has softened and lip products no longer sit well
  • You want subtle fullness and hydration, not a dramatic change
  • You are considering a fuller look as part of overall facial balancing

Your consultation is where this gets decided properly. We will review your medical history, any previous filler, and what is realistic for your lip tissue, and we will be honest if a different approach, such as a lip flip, would suit you better.

Lip Filler FAQs

How long do the results last?

Most results last around 6 to 12 months, depending on the product used, your metabolism and how much movement the area gets. At your consultation we will talk through what to expect for your lips and when a maintenance visit may make sense.

Will my results look natural?

Yes, when it is placed thoughtfully and in the right amount. Lip enhancement here is planned around your natural lip shape and facial balance, so the goal is soft definition and hydration that still looks like you, never overfilled.

What is the difference between lip filler and a lip flip?

Lip filler adds volume, shape and hydration using a hyaluronic acid gel placed directly in the lips. A lip flip uses a small amount of neurotoxin above the upper lip to relax the muscle and gently roll the lip outward. They do different things, and some clients are better suited to one than the other after an in-person assessment.

Is there any downtime?

Downtime is usually minimal. Some swelling and tenderness for a few days is normal, and occasional bruising can happen. Most clients return to normal routines the same day, following the aftercare guidance given at the appointment.
