About the treatment

What Lip Filler Actually Does, and What It Does Not

Lip filler is a smooth gel made from hyaluronic acid, a substance your body produces naturally. Placed precisely into the lip, it does three things at once: it restores structure where the lip has thinned, it refines the border and shape, and it attracts water, which is why treated lips usually feel softer and less dry, not just fuller.

What it does not do is change who you look like. The overfilled look that worries most first-time clients comes from too much product, placed too fast, in the wrong layer. That is a technique problem, not a product problem. Our approach is deliberately conservative: place a little, assess, adjust. You can always add more at a follow-up. Taking it away is a different conversation entirely, although hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved if ever needed, which is one reason we work with them.

We use lip-specific products, including options designed purely for the lips such as Revanesse Lips+, because lip tissue moves constantly and needs a softer, more flexible gel than the cheek or jawline does. Product choice is part of your plan, and we will explain it in plain language before anything is opened.

The Three Things Lip Enhancement Does Best

Nearly every lip appointment in Summerville comes down to one or more of these goals. Naming yours clearly is what turns a generic treatment into a good result.

Definition and Shape

When the lip border softens with age, lipstick bleeds, the cupid's bow flattens and the whole mouth looks less finished. A small amount of filler placed along the border restores that crisp outline, the way a frame finishes a picture, without adding obvious volume.

Balance and Symmetry

No face is perfectly symmetrical, and the lips show it first: one side fuller, the top lip much thinner than the bottom, a corner that pulls down. Correcting that takes deliberately uneven placement, a little more here, a little less there, until the lips sit in balance with each other and with the rest of your features.

Hydration and Fine Lines

Vertical lines above and within the lips are often a structure problem disguised as a skin problem. Because hyaluronic acid draws in water, filler can soften those lines from underneath and help the lip surface hold moisture, so lips look healthier on days you wear nothing at all.

How Much Filler Do You Actually Need?

This is the question clients ask most, and the one with the most misleading answers online. A full syringe holds about a fifth of a teaspoon. Spread across both lips, that is a modest amount. Here is how we usually frame the choice.

Half Syringe: Subtle and Safe to Try

Best for first-time clients, smaller or thinner lips, and anyone whose goal is hydration and definition rather than visible volume. Think of it as your lips on their best day. It is also the right starting point if you are nervous: living with a subtle result for a few months teaches you exactly what you want next time.

Full Syringe: A Noticeable, Balanced Change

Best when you want a clearer change in shape or fullness, when your lips have lost significant volume with age, or when we are correcting asymmetry that needs more structure. Even a full syringe, placed well, should read as natural. If your goals need more than that, we stage it over two appointments rather than forcing it into one.

Pricing depends on the product selected and the amount used, and we confirm the full cost with you before treatment begins. No surprises, no pressure to decide on the day if you would rather think it over. That is what the consultation is for.

Is it right for you

Who This Treatment Suits, and Who Should Wait

You are likely a good candidate if:

  • Your lips have thinned or lost definition as the years have passed
  • One lip, or one side, has always been smaller than the other
  • Your upper lip seems to disappear when you smile
  • You want subtle fullness and hydration, not a dramatic transformation
  • You are planning overall facial balancing and the lips are part of the picture
good to know

Not sure whether you need a half or a full syringe? That is exactly what the consultation decides, with your lips in front of us and zero obligation to proceed on the day.

And we will ask you to wait if you have an active cold sore, a skin infection near the mouth, or very recent dental work, because treating through any of those raises risk without adding benefit. If you get cold sores, tell us at booking: there are simple preventive steps we can plan around your appointment. Honest timing is part of safe treatment.

Your visit

What Happens at Your Appointment

First, the conversation. We look at your lips at rest and in motion, ask what bothers you and what you definitely do not want, review your history and any previous filler, and agree on a plan, including the amount. If a different treatment would serve you better, such as a lip flip for a gummy smile line, we will say so.

Then, the treatment. Your lips are numbed, and the filler is placed in small, measured amounts, checking shape and symmetry as we go rather than at the end. Most appointments finish within the hour, and you will see the new shape immediately, underneath some expected swelling.

Afterwards, the settle. Swelling peaks in the first couple of days and is usually gone within the week. We give you clear aftercare in writing, and we judge the true result together at about two weeks. If a small refinement is needed, that is when we discuss it, with settled lips in front of us rather than swollen ones.

Lip Filler FAQs

How long do lip filler results last?

For most people, results last about 6 to 12 months. The lips move constantly when you talk, eat and smile, so filler breaks down a little faster there than in still areas of the face. Your metabolism, the product used and the amount placed all play a part. At your consultation we will give you a realistic maintenance timeline for your lips, so there are no surprises later.

Will my lips look natural after filler?

That is the entire goal at Contour & Co. Natural results come from three things: the right product for lip tissue, conservative amounts, and placement that respects your existing shape and facial proportions. We would rather build gradually over two visits than overfill in one, and we will tell you honestly if the look you are picturing would not suit your features.

How much filler do I need: half a syringe or a full syringe?

A full syringe sounds like a lot, but it holds only about a fifth of a teaspoon. A half syringe usually gives subtle hydration and definition, which suits first-time clients and smaller lips. A full syringe allows a more noticeable change in shape and volume while still looking balanced. The right amount depends on your starting point and goals, and we decide it together at your consultation, never by upselling.

Does lip filler hurt?

Most clients describe it as brief pinching rather than real pain. The lips are numbed before treatment, and many modern lip fillers also contain lidocaine, so comfort improves as the treatment goes on. The appointment itself is usually under an hour, and any tenderness afterwards typically settles within a few days.

What is the difference between lip filler and a lip flip?

Filler is a hyaluronic acid gel placed in the lips to add shape, structure and hydration, and it lasts 6 to 12 months. A lip flip uses a few units of neurotoxin above the upper lip to relax the muscle so the lip rolls slightly outward, which makes it look fuller without adding volume; it lasts around 2 to 3 months. If your upper lip disappears when you smile, a flip may help. If you want actual volume or definition, filler is the better tool. Some clients combine both, after an in-person assessment.

What should I do before and after my appointment?

Before: arrive with clean skin and tell us about any cold sore history, dental work, medications and previous filler, because these can change the plan. After: expect some swelling for 2 to 4 days, use a cold compress gently, sleep with your head slightly raised the first night, and avoid strenuous exercise, saunas, alcohol and pressing or massaging your lips for the first 24 to 48 hours unless we advise otherwise. Judge your final result at around two weeks, once everything has settled.
